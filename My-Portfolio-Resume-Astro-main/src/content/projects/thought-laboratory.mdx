## Design Approach

<div className="prose prose-slate dark:prose-invert mb-8">
  <p>The feel is quiet, typographic and editorial: long-form passages of original text (including untranslated Chinese script alongside translation) set against a minimal black-and-white canvas, so the ideas carry the page rather than any decoration. An ambient sound toggle lets visitors choose whether to read in silence or with atmosphere.</p>
